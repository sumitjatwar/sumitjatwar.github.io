InCIS 2027 · Track 09

Beyond Access: How Gender and Geography Jointly Shape Digital Payment Inclusion in India

Jatwar, S. K. · Completed research paper · 3rd India Conference on Information Systems

Using Global Findex 2025 microdata on Indian mobile phone owners, the study asks whether gender and rural residence act as separate barriers to active digital payment use. The gender gap is widest among urban women and much smaller in rural India. The paper also looks at how exposure to digital-safety threats relates to usage.

Do Fans Trust the Machine? Acceptance of Automated and Human Video Refereeing Decisions in the FIFA World Cup

Jatwar, S. K. · European Conference on Information Systems

The study compares how fans accept machine-measured decisions, such as offside and goal-line calls, with human video judgement calls, such as penalties and handballs, across recent FIFA World Cups.
